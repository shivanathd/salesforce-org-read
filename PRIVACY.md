# Privacy policy: Mindcat Org Read for Salesforce

Effective 18 October 2026. Publisher: Mindcat Technology Consultants FZE, Dubai. Contact: apps@mindcat.ai.

## What the plugin reads
Setup and usage data from the Salesforce org you sign in to: the org's Id and name, user and licence counts, last-login counts, flows, Workflow Rules, triggers, validation rule counts, Apex test results, coverage and API versions, permission and admin counts, named credentials, connected apps, installed packages, async job counts, custom field names, security settings and org limits. It reads this with your own Salesforce login, through the Salesforce CLI or Salesforce's hosted MCP server.

## Where it runs and who processes the results
Everything runs in your own AI client (Claude Code, Cowork, Claude apps or Cursor) and on your own machine. Query results are processed by Salesforce, which runs your org and the hosted MCP server, and by the AI provider you chose for your client, which reads the results in the conversation. Each does so under its own terms with you.

## What is stored
The plugin writes nothing to disk unless you ask Claude to save the one-page note. Your Salesforce login is stored by the Salesforce CLI or your AI client, not by the plugin.

## What Mindcat receives
Nothing. No data goes to Mindcat. The plugin has no Mindcat server, sends no data to Mindcat, and has no analytics or telemetry. Mindcat cannot see your data, your files or your use of the plugin. The note ends with a short brief of counts and outcomes; it reaches Mindcat only if you choose to send it yourself, and is then handled under the mindcat.ai privacy policy.

## Third parties
The plugin adds no third party of its own. Queries go from your machine or AI client to your own Salesforce org, through Salesforce's own services, and the results stay in your AI client's conversation.

## Retention and deletion
Mindcat retains nothing, because it receives nothing. Files the plugin writes are on your disk; delete them when you like. Uninstall the plugin with `/plugin uninstall`.

## Children
The plugin is for businesses and is not intended for anyone under 18.
